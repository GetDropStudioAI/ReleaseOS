# Gap analysis: "Comprehensive Guide to Release Management" vs ReleaseOS

Baseline: `main@e90b24d` (2026-10-21). Read-only analysis; nothing in the repo was changed.
Guide: "Comprehensive Guide to Release Management" (supplied by the product owner, 2026-10-21; not checked in) (§1 fundamentals, §2 runbook template, §3 ticket ownership, §4 tiers, §5 Option A).

## Summary

- The app already covers most of the guide's mechanics: gates with DB-enforced lockout and SoD, timed runbooks with forecast and a rollback deadline, rehearsals, Go/No-Go with expiring conditions, freezes, change record, T-minus comms, PIR, and an evidence pack.
- The gaps fall into five groups:
  1. **The intake / Definition-of-Ready checklist is not reusable.** Templates cannot carry checklist tasks.
  2. **Some intake items are not measured at all:** contingency %, artifact immutability, and telemetry and bridge links.
  3. **The war-room layer is thin:** no bridge link, no roster with backups, and broadcasts are not tied to the runbook.
  4. **Rollback and retro follow-through is incomplete:** no PIR on a rolled-back run or an aborted train, and no PIR due date.
  5. **Metrics:** there is no time-to-restore, and the change-failure rate ignores aborted trains.
- **Found in passing (not from the guide):** the web UI cannot complete a train with a close code, or abort a train. The header's "Advance to Complete" always gets 422 `CloseCodeRequired`. This is a prerequisite for several items below.
- **Six guide items would contradict a confirmed decision** (D10, D11, D14, D25, and the rejected alternatives on inbound webhooks and ServiceNow write-back). Do not build these unless the owner reverses the decision.
